🌿 The Science of Weather & Mood

  • ☀️ Sunlight & Serotonin: Morning sunlight stimulates the optic nerve, instructing your hypothalamus to boost serotonin and stabilize mood.
  • 🌧️ Negative Ions in Rain: Raindrops colliding with the ground generate high concentrations of negative ions, scientifically linked to reduced anxiety.
  • ⛅ Cloudy Comfort: Mild, cloud-covered days decrease sensory overstimulation, making it an ideal environment for introspection and journaling.
  • 💧 Hydration & Fatigue: Even mild dehydration (1-2%) increases perceived fatigue and impairs working memory. Drink fresh water consistently.
